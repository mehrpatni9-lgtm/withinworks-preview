WithinWorks LeadershipTypography and direction

Two typefaces, one warm accent, nothing else

Primary · headings and the wordmark

Fraunces

A high contrast serif with warmth in the letterforms. Every heading, the wordmark, pull quotes and numbers. Weight 500, italic for emphasis on a single phrase.

ABCDEFGHIJKLM abcdefghijklm 0123456789

Secondary · everything else

Jost

A geometric sans, set light at weight 300. Body copy, navigation, labels and buttons. Small uppercase with wide letter spacing for section labels.

ABCDEFGHIJKLM abcdefghijklm 0123456789

A warm, grounded palette that reads as credible without performing. Premium, never showy.

  • Ivory ground throughout, warm rather than white, so nothing feels clinical
  • Sage carries the brand. The heading colour, the button colour and the mark
  • Mocha is the only accent. Used sparingly, never behind long text
  • Deep forest bands break the page and carry the quotes
  • Fraunces for character, Jost for everything that has to be read
  • Restraint is the brand. When in doubt, take something out

WithinWorks LeadershipTone of voice

How it should sound

Warm, reflective and grounded. Strategic without being corporate. She is talking to someone senior, in private.

Do

  • Write the way she speaks. One idea per sentence.
  • Lead with the person, not the process.
  • Name the specific thing. A leader who stopped sleeping beats a leader under pressure.
  • Let short sentences stand on their own.
  • Keep the quotes marked verbatim in the brand brief exactly as written.

Do not

  • No superlatives, no transform your life, no unlock your potential.
  • No exclamation marks.
  • No em dashes anywhere.
  • No invented client stories or testimonials.
  • No corporate jargon. If a word would not survive being said out loud, cut it.
