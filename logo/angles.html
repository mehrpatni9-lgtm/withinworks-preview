Six new angles

None of these is an abstract symbol sitting in a shape. The old set was geometry, which is what every coaching brand reaches for, and the enclosing circle is what made the monogram read medical.

Leadership

Angle 01

No symbol at all

Within in light italic, Works in heavy roman. The contrast between the two halves is the idea. This is what senior advisory firms do, and it cannot look generic because it is her actual name.

WithinWorks

Angle 02

The held space

Two brackets holding one point. Straight out of her own line, I do not give advice, I create a space. The space between them is the product.

Angle 03

The counter

A heavy W where the shape held inside the middle is picked out in mocha. The mark is the inside of the letter, which is as close to the word within as a letterform gets.

Angle 04

One letter, carried

A single serif W doing the heavy lifting with the name stacked beside it. Asymmetric, so it never reads as a badge or a seal. Works as an avatar on its own.

WithinWorks

Angle 05

The pause

Two strokes of different length, the typographic mark for a pause. Her whole method is asking the question then staying quiet. Nothing else in coaching looks like this.

Angle 06

Two Ws, offset

The monogram you liked, with the circle taken off and the second W shifted so it is no longer symmetrical. The circle and the symmetry together were the medical part.
