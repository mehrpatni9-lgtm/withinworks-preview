The split

The idea

One letter divided down the middle. The left half is Within, the right half is Works, and the line between them is where the two meet.

Why this one

It carries the whole proposition inside a single letterform, so there is no symbol to explain and nothing extra to shrink. The one you already liked, kept as the benchmark.

The inner line

The idea

A fine mocha line dropped from the top of the letter down into the space held inside it. The movement is inward, not outward.

Why this one

Transformation happens from the inside out is the brand line, and this is the only mark that draws the direction of travel. Reads as deliberate, never decorative.

The pause

The idea

A W built from two strokes with the join taken out. The letter is complete but there is a held gap where the two halves would meet.

Why this one

Her method is asking the question and then staying quiet. This is that silence, built into the letter. Distinctive without being abstract, and the gap survives at small sizes.

Nested

The idea

Three Vs held one inside another, tightening toward a mocha centre. From a distance the silhouette is a W.

Why this one

Within, drawn literally, as one thing sitting inside another. The eye travels to the centre on its own, which is exactly where her work points.

The threshold

The idea

An arch standing inside an arch. A doorway, with a second doorway held within it.

Why this one

Straight from her own essay, Reinvention Isn't a Crisis, It's a Threshold. The most architectural option, and the most adult. It would look right carved into a wall.

The counter

The idea

A heavy W with the shape trapped inside the middle vee picked out in mocha. The mark is the inside of the letter rather than the letter.

Why this one

The strongest performer from the first round. It is the only earlier mark that held its idea all the way down to twenty pixels, so it earns its place next to the split.
