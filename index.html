About

I have stood on both sides of this

For most of my career my job was the architecture. Fourteen years in global banking, then Chief People Officer and founding team at Traveazy Group, which we took from an idea to more than 350 people, through a pandemic and two mergers.

None of that is what I remember. What I remember are the conversations after the meeting ended. The leader who had stopped sleeping. The founder who could feel that the instincts which built the company were the wrong ones to scale it. Those conversations were never on the agenda, and they were always the ones that mattered.

So I do not give advice. I have opinions and I keep most of them to myself, because the answer you reach yourself is the only one you will act on. My work is to ask the question you have been circling, then stay quiet long enough for you to get there.

Outside the room: two daughters, 21 and 16, an unreasonable love of dogs, 45 countries and counting, and a yoga and meditation practice I have kept for a long time. The groundedness is not a technique I learned for the work.

Questions

Before you book

Is the discovery call really complimentary?

Yes. Forty five minutes, no charge and nothing sold. It exists so both of us can tell whether the work is right before anyone commits.

Do you work with people outside the UAE?

Yes. The work is online, so location is not a constraint. Clients join from wherever they are.

What is the difference between coaching and advice?

Advice hands you a conclusion. Coaching creates the conditions for you to reach your own, which is the one you will actually act on.

Is this therapy?

No. Coaching is forward facing and works with capable people at inflection points. It is not clinical treatment.

What does the six-session journey cost?

Pricing is discussed on the discovery call, once the shape of the work is clear.

How confidential is this?

Completely. Nothing said in a session leaves it, and no client is named publicly without their permission.
